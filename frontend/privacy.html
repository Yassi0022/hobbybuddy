Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 7, 2026

1. Introduction

HobbyBuddy ("we", "our", or "us") is a social platform designed to connect individuals based on shared interests and psychological compatibility. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal data in compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Italian privacy laws. For the purposes of the GDPR, HobbyBuddy acts as the Data Controller.

2. Data We Collect

To provide our matching services and community features, we collect the following personal data:

  • Account Information: Email address, password (encrypted), and display name.
  • Profile Information: Age, city, profile photos (URLs), and three Hinge-style icebreaker prompts.
  • Psychological Data: Personality trait scores derived from the IPIP Big-Five assessment (Openness, Conscientiousness, Extraversion, Agreeableness, Emotional Stability).
  • Social Content: Hobbies, community posts, event participation data, and private messages between users.

3. How We Use Your Data

We process your information for the following purposes:

  • Matching Algorithm: To calculate compatibility scores and suggest potential "hobby twins."
  • Profile Visibility: To display your identity card, hobbies, and prompts to other users on the platform.
  • Community Features: To enable the social feed, event organization, and messaging functionality.
  • Platform Improvement: To analyze usage patterns and improve our matching accuracy.

4. Legal Basis (GDPR Art. 6)

We process your data based on:

  • Consent: Your explicit agreement when you register and complete the personality quiz.
  • Legitimate Interest: To maintain and improve the core functionality and security of the HobbyBuddy platform.

5. Data Sharing and Storage

We do NOT sell your personal data to third parties. Your data is stored securely using Aiven (MySQL Cloud) infrastructure. We may share data only with service providers strictly necessary for platform operation (e.g., hosting and database management) who are also compliant with GDPR standards.

6. Your Rights (GDPR)

As a user in the European Union/Italy, you have the following rights:

  • Access: Request a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Rectification: Correct any inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Erasure: Request the deletion of your account and associated data ("Right to be Forgotten").
  • Portability: Request that we transfer your data to another service.
  • Objection: Object to the processing of your data for specific purposes.

To exercise these rights, please contact us at contact@hobbybuddy.app.

7. Data Retention

Your data is retained as long as your account is active. If you request account deletion, all personal data, including quiz results and messages, will be permanently deleted from our active databases within 30 days.

8. IPIP Attribution

The personality assessment used on HobbyBuddy is based on the International Personality Item Pool (IPIP) Big-Five model. For more information, visit ipip.ori.org.

9. Contact

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact:

Email: contact@hobbybuddy.app
